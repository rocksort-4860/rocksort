Privacy Policy · RockSort

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Overview

RockSort is a local-first task-management iOS application. This privacy policy explains how information is handled within the app. RockSort does NOT require you to register an account, and we do not collect, upload or send your task-related data to any external server. All user-created task content is stored locally only on your device.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number or any personally-identifiable information.
  • We do not collect task text, priority settings, completion records or any content you create inside RockSort.
  • We do not integrate analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, advertising or user-tracking frameworks. No usage-behavior data is sent out of your device.
  • We have no backend server receiving your private app-generated content.

Data Stored Locally On Your Device

All your tasks, completion history, statistics, app preferences and in-app purchase receipt cache are saved inside the local sandbox storage of your iOS device. This information never leaves your device and is inaccessible to us as developers.

  • Task entries, importance / urgency ratings, due dates, reminder settings.
  • Completed-task records, streak-day counters and local app-setting values.
  • Local cache of App-Store in-app-purchase receipt for recognizing your Pro-version entitlement.

Uninstalling RockSort will permanently erase all locally-stored app-related data on your device.

Data Storage and iCloud Backup

RockSort is a local-only application. We do not collect, transmit, or store your task data on any remote servers. If you enable system-level iCloud Backup on your iOS device, your local application data may be encrypted and backed up to your personal iCloud account by Apple. This process is entirely managed by Apple, and we have no access to your personal data.

Local Notifications

RockSort may request permission for local notifications. Reminder schedules are stored entirely on-device. Reminder-related data will not be transmitted to any remote server. You are able to enable or disable notification permissions at any time inside iOS System-Settings.

In-App Purchases (Apple App Store)

Pro-feature subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed entirely by Apple App Store. RockSort itself cannot access your payment credentials, Apple-ID account or billing information. Purchase-related transactions are governed by Apple's own privacy policy. Please refer to Apple Privacy Policy for payment-related details.

Purchase entitlement verification is assisted by RevenueCat, a third-party payment and entitlement service. Transaction and receipt data are processed in accordance with Apple's and RevenueCat's respective privacy policies; RevenueCat does not receive your task content or any personal information.

Third-Party Services

RockSort integrates RevenueCat solely for App Store in-app-purchase receipt verification and Pro-entitlement recognition. RevenueCat only processes purchase/transaction records required to validate your subscription and does not receive your task content or personal data. We do not embed any SDK for analytics, advertising, crash-tracking or social login.

Children's Privacy

RockSort is not specifically targeted for children under 13-years-old. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from minors.

Changes To This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Updated content will be published on this page with a revised "last-updated" date. Continued usage of RockSort after changes means you accept the revised policy.

概述

RockSort 是一款优先本地运行的 iOS 任务管理 App。本隐私政策说明 App 内部的数据处理规则。RockSort 不需要注册账号；我们不会收集、上传、向外发送你的任何任务相关数据。你创建的全部任务内容仅保存在你本机设备本地。

我们不会收集的数据

  • 不会收集你的姓名、邮箱、手机号等任何个人身份信息。
  • 不会收集你输入的任务文本、优先级设置、完成记录以及你在 App 内产生的任何内容。
  • App 不集成统计、崩溃上报、广告、用户追踪类第三方 SDK，不会将你的使用行为数据传出设备。
  • 不存在后端服务器接收你的 App 私有内容。

仅保存在你设备本地的数据

你的全部任务、完成历史、统计数据、App 设置、内购凭证缓存全部保存在 iOS 设备沙盒本地存储中。开发者无法访问这些本地存储内容。

  • 任务条目、重要/紧急等级、截止日期、提醒配置。
  • 已完成任务记录、连续天数统计、App 各项偏好设置。
  • App Store 内购凭证本地缓存，用于识别你是否拥有专业版权益。

卸载 RockSort App，会永久清除设备上该 App 的全部本地数据。

数据存储与 iCloud 备份说明

RockSort 是一款纯本地运行的应用，我们不会在任何远程服务器上收集、传输或存储您的任务数据。当您在 iOS 设备上启用了系统级 iCloud 整机备份或 App 备份功能时，您保存在本设备上的应用数据将由 Apple 系统加密备份至您的个人 iCloud 账户中。该备份完全由 Apple 管理，我们无法访问或获取您的任何个人数据。

本地通知

RockSort 会向你申请本地通知权限。所有提醒计划全部存储在本机，提醒相关数据不会上传到任何远程服务器。你随时可以在 iOS「设置」中开启或关闭通知权限。

App Store 应用内购买

专业版订阅与买断均完全由 Apple App Store 处理。RockSort 本身无法获取你的支付卡号、Apple ID 账号、账单信息。交易相关规则请参阅苹果官方隐私政策：Apple 隐私政策。

专业版权益的购买凭证校验由 RevenueCat（第三方支付与权益服务）协助处理，相关交易与回执数据依照 Apple 与 RevenueCat 各自的隐私政策处理；RevenueCat 不会收到你的任务内容或任何个人信息。

第三方服务

RockSort 仅集成 RevenueCat 用于 App Store 内购凭证校验与专业版权益识别。RevenueCat 仅处理用于验证订阅所需的购买/交易记录，不会收到你的任务内容或个人数据。我们未嵌入任何用于统计、广告、崩溃追踪或社交登录的 SDK。

儿童隐私

本应用并非面向 13 周岁以下儿童设计，我们不会刻意收集未成年人个人信息。
