Frequently asked questions

常见问题

Q: How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Q：如何恢复已购买的 Pro 权益？

Open the Pro screen in the App and tap “Restore Purchase”. The entitlement is restored through your Apple Account — you do not need to pay again.

在 App 内进入「Pro / 专业版」页面，点击 「恢复购买」。权益会通过你的 Apple 账户恢复，无需重复付费。

Q: How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Q：如何管理或取消订阅？

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to iOS Settings → [your Apple ID] → Subscriptions to view, renew, or cancel at any time. Cancellation stops future renewals; it does not refund the current period already paid.

订阅由 Apple 管理。请在 iOS「设置 → 你的 Apple ID → 订阅」中随时查看、续费或取消。取消仅阻止后续续费，已支付的当期费用不予退还。

Q: Where is my data stored? Can it be lost?

Q：我的数据保存在哪里？会丢失吗？

All your tasks and completion records are stored locally on your own device. We do not upload or collect them. Uninstalling the App, clearing local storage, or switching to a new device without a backup can make the data unrecoverable — please use the export feature and keep your own copy.

你的全部任务与完成记录都保存在你自己的设备本地，我们不会上传或收集。卸载应用、清除本地存储，或在没有备份的情况下更换设备，可能导致数据无法恢复——请使用导出功能并自行留存副本。

Q: The App crashes or won't open

Q：App 闪退或无法打开

Please email us with your device model and iOS version, and a short description of what you were doing when it happened. If possible, keep the App updated to the latest version from the App Store before reporting.

请发送邮件并附上设备型号与iOS 版本，并简单说明发生问题前你在做什么。报告前，请尽量把 App 更新到 App Store 上的最新版本。

Q: Can I get a refund?

Q：可以退款吗？

All in‑app purchases are settled through the Apple App Store, and refunds follow Apple policy. Apple handles all refund requests — you can submit one through reportaproblem.apple.com. We support Apple's decision.

所有应用内购买均通过 Apple App Store 结算，退款按 Apple 政策处理。退款申请由 Apple 统一处理，可通过 reportaproblem.apple.com 提交。我们对 Apple 作出的退款决定予以支持。

Q: Will you use my data for ads?

Q：你们会拿我的数据做广告吗？

No. RockSort shows no ads and we do not sell your data. Your task content stays on your device and is not uploaded anywhere. To process and restore purchases, our third‑party purchase service (RevenueCat, with Apple handling payment) receives limited purchase and technical information as described in the Privacy Policy — not your task content, and not for advertising.

不会。RockSort 不投放广告，我们也不出售你的数据。你的任务内容保存在你的设备上，不会被上传到任何地方。为处理与恢复购买，我们的第三方内购服务（RevenueCat，支付由 Apple 处理）会接收有限的购买与技术信息，详见《隐私政策》——不涉及你的任务内容，也不会用于广告。
