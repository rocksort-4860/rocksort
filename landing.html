Philosophy

Make "most important first" a discipline you can actually keep

No willpower required — just one routine you can repeat. Three minutes a day, and the day tilts toward what matters.

01

Write it down

Only six tasks today, and once the list is full, that is it. The moment it is written down, most of the noise has already been filtered out.

02

Set the weights

Tag each task for importance and urgency, and the list sorts itself. What you do first is decided by the rules — not by how you feel.

Features

Trimmed down to the core — and the core, done properly

No calendar, no team chat, no check-in community. RockSort answers exactly one question: what do I do first today?

Weighted sorting

Score every task for importance and urgency, and RockSort orders the list for you — so you stop guessing what to do first.

Six a day, then stop

The list caps out at six. A smaller list means your attention finally stops being diluted across twenty open tabs.

Start with the biggest rock

Finish the first task before opening the second. Your best energy goes to the work that matters, not the work that is easiest.

Weekly review

RockSort keeps the score for you. See how the week actually went, share it in one tap, and stay honest about it.

Local and private

Everything lives on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is tracked, and deleting the app leaves nothing behind.

Deliberately minimal

No ads, no social feed, no extra buttons. Open it, write the list, start the first task.
